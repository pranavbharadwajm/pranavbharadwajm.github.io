About Me

I spent two years working as a test automation engineer, pulling apart complex systems to find exactly where they break. Today, I am formalizing my pivot into business strategy as an MSc Management student at UCD.

I realized that writing efficient code is only half the battle; the real challenge is figuring out if you are building the right thing to begin with. I combine a technical foundation with a deep interest in financial mechanics and product strategy. When I am not tearing down business models, you can usually find me in the football field or at the movies.
